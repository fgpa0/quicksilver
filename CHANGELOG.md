# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-07)
First public release.
- VBO terrain renderer, threaded chunk meshing, cave culling, GPU time of day.
- Progressive chunk loading, background world generation and decoration, faster lighting, async chunk IO.
- Frame rate cap, VSync and monitor-matched frame pacing.
- Built-in shader pack support (Video Settings > Shaders...). Only Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3 is supported for
  now.
- Settings screen under Video Settings > Quicksilver...
